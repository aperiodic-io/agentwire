01 · Two layers

Squeeze the wire. Shrink the context.

Use them separately or together. The transport never touches your payloads. The reducer only touches what you've explicitly marked as low priority, and it can always restore it.

● byte-exact

Transport

Frames and compresses streams of opaque binary messages, optionally with a shared, versioned Zstd dictionary. The other side reconstructs the exact bytes, IDs, kinds and order.

  • AWC1 keeps one Zstd stream per connection, so each message compresses against everything sent before it
  • AWF1 sends self-contained frames for storage, fan-out and replay
  • AWD1 sends only the change when a client re-sends a growing context
◐ lossy in the prompt, recoverable from the archive

Context reducer

Replaces low-priority tool results with a short preview and a SHA-256 reference. The full payload goes to a compressed, content-addressed archive first, so you can restore it exactly.

  • Instructions, tool calls, state and errors are never touched
  • Deterministic: the largest savings are applied first, and it stops once the budget is met
  • Raises BudgetExceeded instead of silently dropping anything

This compresses for real, in your browser. It uses the built-in CompressionStream (deflate, 32 KiB window) because browsers don't ship zstd. Each stream message is costed as the growth of the compressed history plus a 5 B flush marker, and each format's frame header is added: 56 B for AWF1, 10 B for AWC1. AgentWire uses libzstd with up to an 8 MiB window, so the absolute numbers differ, but the shape is the same.

06 · Pick a mode

The right tool for your traffic.

Each recommendation is based on measurements, not guesses. Pick the situation that matches yours.

07 · Fail closed

It never guesses.

If a dictionary is missing or wrong, a frame is corrupt or oversized, or a sequence has a gap, the decoder raises an exception. It never hands you guessed data.

DictionaryMismatch

Exact dictionary identity

Every frame or RESET carries a dictionary version and its SHA-256 fingerprint. The decoder never falls back to a different dictionary.

ProtocolError

Bounds checked before decompressing

Sizes are validated before anything is buffered, and output is capped at the declared length. A peer can't make you allocate unbounded memory.

SequenceError

Gaps and replays caught

AWF1 sequence numbers must be contiguous. Repeated, missing or reordered frames are rejected.

SessionPoisoned

No half-trusted state

After any AWC1 error, the decoder's state is unknown, so every later call raises. Recovery means reconnecting with empty history.

BudgetExceeded

No silent drops

If the budget can't be met without touching protected events, the reducer refuses rather than cutting anything important.

ArchiveCorruptError

Verified restores

The archive checks the length and the full SHA-256 of every blob before returning it.
